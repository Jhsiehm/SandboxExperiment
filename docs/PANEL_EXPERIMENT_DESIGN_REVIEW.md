# First linked-panel experiment: independent design review

2026-10-23. Documentation/design only. No new respondent data, outcome values, model calls or training were accessed. This review uses the existing feasibility inventory and research-plan sections 4, 7 and the longitudinal ablation proposal. It does not certify a harmonized endpoint or adequate sample size.

## Concrete first experiment

Use the linked ANES 2016–2020–2024 panel and one candidate construct: seven-category party identification, subject to verified wording, branching, order and pre-election phase across all three years. Party identification measures partisan affiliation, not generic policy opinion; conclusions must retain that distinction. Develop on observed 2016 pre → 2020 pre responses, freeze one predictor, then evaluate 2020 pre → 2024 pre. Do not mix pre/post occasions to increase sample size. If harmonization fails, stop this endpoint and record the reason before selecting a separately versioned alternative.

Retain the **raw Laplace-one initial-state transition baseline**: for K verified ordered categories, count development transitions N[i,j], then predict (N[i,j]+1)/(sum_j N[i,j]+K). For seven-category party ID, K=7; do not copy six-category DEBATE constants. No learned bias, temperature or calibrated residual replaces this reference. Persistence is a separate secondary comparator. The earlier DEBATE calibrated control remains a development diagnostic and contributes neither parameters nor evidence of panel validity.

The authorized first stage is baseline-only ingestion/readiness assessment, with candidate and practical margin explicitly unresolved. It does not run an enhanced predictor. A later primary contrast should be a single locked enhanced predictor against this raw transition reference on exactly the same eligible people. A modest interpretable candidate is a regularized multinomial transition model using current party category plus a small metadata-approved set of current non-target responses. Candidate feature names, missingness encoding, transformations, optimizer, regularization and fallback behavior must be specified before its fitting; they are not determined by this review. Prefer this bounded data test before adding local neural training or social interaction. Until candidate and features are specified, readiness is **design/preparation ready, evaluation blocked**, not an executable experiment.

A baseline-only plumbing pass is useful for checking ingestion and scorer correctness but is not evidence that a candidate improves prediction. Do not inspect 2024 target frequencies for that pass.

## Temporal and person separation

All rows belonging to one linked person must stay together in development partitioning and uncertainty calculations. Partition development people deterministically using the stable linked ID and a committed salt before target access. Use a fixed five-fold person-level development split for fitting/tuning assessment, subject to enough observed categories per training partition. This is cross-person validation of one historical transition, not five temporal replications. Document household/PSU dependencies where supplied and keep related sampling units together when required by the design.

The primary future task may reuse the development people in 2020→2024. It then measures later-wave prediction for known panel members. Their observed 2020 state is a legitimate input for a forecast initialized in 2020 even though it was a development target. It must never be described as transfer to previously unseen people. For cleaner unseen-person evidence, reserve a disjoint ID cohort before any fitting and keep its 2020 development labels unused; evaluate it separately in 2024. Do not add that smaller secondary cohort by default if it would leave the primary study underpowered. Choose and freeze the estimand and cohort policy first.

A modern model or later-released survey file makes this retrospective temporal transfer, not an operational forecast actually available in 2020. Fixed world-event or candidate features must carry explicit time availability rules. No 2024 party label, vote, post-election response, later text, derived variable incorporating later responses, or future imputation information may enter a predictor.

## The early-history limitation

The early training pair supplies one input wave and one target wave. It cannot train or validate a feature that requires two earlier observed party-ID waves before predicting a third. Adding 2016 party ID only to the 2020→2024 candidate changes its information/schema relative to the development task. That is not a matched history ablation and should not be the primary experiment.

Same-wave non-target responses at the input occasion are potentially available in both transitions and can support a matched richer-profile test after field-level crosswalk validation. Call them contemporaneous profile responses, not longitudinal history. Maintenance surveys cannot repair the history limitation until the exact target and feature questions are verified at those occasions. Do not infer annual observations or fabricate interpolated labels. A genuine earlier-history test requires additional verified earlier observations or a separate explicitly justified protocol.

## Primary score and meaningful improvement

Use normalized ordinal ranked probability score: average squared cumulative-probability error across K−1 ordered cuts. The paired primary estimand is mean RPS(reference) minus mean RPS(candidate), positive favoring the candidate. Freeze whether the primary population is the eligible panel cohort with equal person weights or a documentation-supported survey-weighted population; report the other as a labeled secondary diagnostic. Never silently change the estimand after attrition.

No numeric practical margin is adopted for this panel protocol. As an illustration only, a threshold of 0.01 normalized RPS would mean: one percentage point of the full 0–1 loss range. It is **not** justified merely because the DEBATE experiment used 0.01, and does not mean one percentage point more correct answers. Before evaluation, justify and freeze a margin by a concrete downstream use/loss requirement or a clearly labeled pilot decision rule. The previous DEBATE margin is not inherited. Do not lower it because a development power calculation is unfavorable. Freeze the distinction between practical and statistical success: a useful rule is point improvement at least the fixed practical minimum plus a design-appropriate one-sided 95% lower confidence bound above zero. That supports a positive effect with the selected practical point estimate, not confidence that the true effect itself exceeds 0.01; the stronger latter claim requires the bound above 0.01.

Use development-only paired loss simulations/resampling at the independent sampling-unit level to estimate detectable effects, interval widths and expected eligible sample sizes. Vary plausible attrition scenarios without opening 2024 outcomes. Report sensitivity to weight concentration and repeated-person/PSU dependence. A nominal respondent count is not effective sample size; a simulation is conditional on its assumed effect and dependence structure. Insufficient precision means inconclusive, not equivalence or proof of no signal.

Secondary diagnostics should include persistence, change-event Brier score, per-initial-category RPS and calibration, observed/predicted change rates, and weighted/unweighted estimates. Keep these diagnostic rather than selecting a candidate on the best subgroup or metric. Candidate failures must not remove hard cases from its denominator; predeclare deterministic fallback to the raw baseline or count the run as failed.

## Missingness, stability and attrition

The merged release universe already conditions on participation through at least 2020 pre. Thus even the early transition is not a representative 2016-only cohort by default. Document this selection explicitly. Define source eligibility using wave-specific question universe and routing, keep refusal/don't-know/not-asked distinct, and never map missing codes to the ideological midpoint. Freeze whether the primary complete-observed target estimand excludes target nonresponse or treats a verified substantive category separately. Report every exclusion count by reason and wave.

Any imputation, missing-feature indicator, category pooling or scaling must be fitted inside development training partitions. Target missingness is not an invitation to impute evaluation truth. Report 2024 retention by permitted pre-2024 characteristics and initial category, and contrast the retained evaluation cohort with the initial eligible cohort. This can reveal selective attrition but cannot prove missing outcomes are ignorable.

Weights must match the linked cohort, occasion and stated estimand. Do not apply a final-wave panel weight to 2016→2020 simply because it exists. Verify applicable pre/post longitudinal weights, PSU/stratum fields, normalization and uncertainty guidance before a population claim. Unknown suitable weights block population inference, not reversible schema preparation or clearly labeled unweighted cohort analysis. Weight adjustment does not remove arbitrary nonresponse bias. Questionnaire wording, branch changes, survey mode and category semantics require a documented stability audit independent of performance.

## Separate one-step, autonomous and assimilation tasks

The primary task is **one-step conditional prediction**: observe authorized 2020 inputs, predict the next measured 2024 state. It makes no claim about intervening annual states or interaction dynamics.

An autonomous diagnostic can propagate the 2016 initial distribution through a development-fitted transition operator twice without observing 2020 outcomes. Applying the same operator twice assumes time-homogeneous transition behavior across two election cycles. A richer-profile model also needs a predeclared way to evolve its non-target inputs; silently replacing those with observed 2020 values would cease to be autonomous. Two intervals provide very limited evidence about long-run error accumulation.

An assimilated diagnostic explicitly ingests a frozen list of observed 2020 measurements before predicting 2024. If that list is exactly the one-step inputs, it is the same prediction task, not an independent success. Evaluate all variants on matched eligible people, disclose information differences and report separately. No social network or interaction treatment is identified by this linked-panel design alone.

## Minimal stage gates

1. **Metadata gate:** frozen release/source digests; verified linked ID uniqueness rules; exact endpoint and feature crosswalk with phases, wording, order, routing, mode and missing codes; applicable license and weights documented. Unknown items are explicit blockers to their dependent stage.
2. **Contract gate:** immutable person partitions, target universe, input whitelist, candidate/reference algorithms, category count, missingness/failure rules, primary estimand/contrast, practical threshold, uncertainty method and evaluation release policy. A trusted evaluator holds later labels separately. Preparation and tests can proceed now; no additional permission ceremony is needed for already-authorized reversible work.
3. **Development gate:** ingestion exposes development labels only; schema and linkage audits, denominator accounting, fitting-only transformations, fixed development resampling and power/precision assessment. Later target availability flags are handled only by the evaluator under a frozen policy. No 2024 frequencies for model selection.
4. **Freeze gate:** one candidate selected without later outcomes; code/environment/model/feature and prediction hashes sealed; baseline uses exactly the authorized development rows; complete paired prediction coverage. Failed development gates stop escalation and preserve artifacts.
5. **Evaluation gate:** evaluator verifies seals, computes the frozen paired scores and design-aware intervals, reports attrition and secondary diagnostics, and records pass/fail/inconclusive without refitting. Any subsequent redesign is a new development version and cannot reuse this result as untouched confirmation.

The immediate next work is metadata-backed schema and readiness-contract preparation. Neither the previous DEBATE failure nor promising panel documentation justifies skipping harmonization, effective-sample assessment or the temporal information boundary.
