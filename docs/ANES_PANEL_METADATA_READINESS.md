# ANES linked-panel metadata readiness — 2026-10-23

The smallest plausible design is seven-category pre-election party identification: develop the transition on 2016→2020 and evaluate 2020→2024. It is **not ready for respondent-data ingestion**: the 2024 summary construction, exact release alignment, and source-byte provenance remain unresolved. This pass read only official documentation, with no respondent datasets or model calls.

## Verified metadata

The linked study includes 2016 pre/post completers who completed at least the 2020 pre survey. Its documented merge key is `V160001_orig`. The 2016 interviews used face-to-face/internet; 2020 and 2024 panel interviews used internet. The cohort is therefore a continuing 2016 population, not a fresh 2024 electorate. The study page identifies a May 19, 2026 release; its linked errata page still names October 30, 2025. Treat this as a provenance discrepancy pending resolution, not interchangeable releases. [Merged study](https://electionstudies.org/data-center/2016-2020-2024-panel-merged-study/), [merged errata](https://electionstudies.org/data-center/2016-2020-2024-panel-merged-study/2016-2020-2024-panel-merged-file-updates-errata/).

Pre-election endpoints are `V161158x` (2016), `V201231x` (2020), and `V241227x` (2024). Each lists the same ordered codes 1–7, from strong Democrat through independent to strong Republican. The documented summary missing codes are -9/-8 in 2016/2020 and -9/-8/-4 in 2024. Anything outside 1–7 must remain missing with its reason preserved; actual merged-file blanks and wave absence have not been inspected. [2016 codebook, printed p256](https://electionstudies.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/anes_timeseries_2016_userguidecodebook.pdf), [2020 codebook, printed p115](https://electionstudies.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/anes_timeseries_2020_userguidecodebook_20220210.pdf), [2024 codebook](https://electionstudies.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/anes_timeseries_2024_userguidecodebook_accessible_html.html).

For the 2016–2020 panel, `V200011a` is the pre-election weight; `b` is post, `c` PSU and `d` stratum. For the 2016–2020–2024 panel, corresponding fields are `V240106a/b/c/d`. The 2020 guidance explicitly targets the 2016 electorate. Use the pre weights for the proposed pre-only endpoints, with a separately frozen plan for valid-response subgroups and survey uncertainty. Weights cannot establish ignorable attrition. [2020 guide, printed pp9–10](https://electionstudies.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/anes_timeseries_2020_userguidecodebook_20220210.pdf), [2024 weighting guidance](https://electionstudies.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/anes_timeseries_2024_userguidecodebook_accessible_html.html).

## Construct blocker

The earlier chain uses party identification, partisan strength, and leaning. The 2020 construction appendix allows a valid leaning answer to yield a valid summary even when the first answer was negative-coded; screening only the first raw item would incorrectly discard these cases. [2020 construction appendix, printed pp772–773](https://electionstudies.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/anes_timeseries_2020_userguidecodebook_20220210.pdf).

In 2024, `V241225` asks web independent nonleaners another proximity question. It is included among `V241227x` source questions alongside the traditional chain and paper item `V241226`. The codebook documents routing and response categories but the reviewed material did not establish the exact summary formula. A focused official-source search did not resolve how this extra answer affects the midpoint. Equal summary labels therefore do not certify equivalent measurement. Paper respondents are outside the documented internet-only 2024 panel, but the web follow-up is directly relevant. Do not silently substitute the paper item or invent a recode. [2024 codebook, V241225–V241227x](https://electionstudies.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/anes_timeseries_2024_userguidecodebook_20260519.pdf).

## Provenance and release blockers

The official repeated-variable link is labeled CSV on the study page but returned ZIP content unsupported by the web reader. Its members and schema remain unverified. Direct documentation downloads returned HTTP 403; no original document bytes were archived and every original SHA is explicitly null. The stored evidence JSON is an agent-authored paraphrase with its own hash, **not** a source-byte freeze. Exact URLs, reported versions, locators, failed requests and statuses are retained under `reports/anes-panel-readiness-20261023/`. [Official repeated-variable documentation link](https://electionstudies.org/repeated_variables_2016_20_24/).

The 2016 PDF carries an internal 20190904 footer despite its older URL path; the 2020 PDF is dated February 10, 2022, while its accessible HTML has an older title. The current 2024 document identifies May 19, 2026. None of these observations proves which component versions the merged file includes. Actual merged column existence/casing, unique linkage, response availability and subgroup sample sizes remain unverified.

## Minimum design to freeze after these blockers clear

Keep the raw Laplace-one 7×7 transition and persistence references. Use initial party ID alone, equal four-year pre/pre horizons, and no unmatched earlier-history feature. Report unweighted predictive performance as the raw reference and define any weighted analysis separately. Freeze attrition/item-missingness accounting before endpoint access. Shared people across transitions support a within-person temporal claim, not an unseen-person claim; a person-held-out question would require a different design. No causal effect claim follows from these observational transitions.

Obtain exact official summary construction or an authoritative documented common-chain harmonization, archive the documentation bytes with hashes, reconcile release versions and inspect only an authorized schema before admitting respondent data. The readiness metadata deliberately leaves those gates closed.
