          Trust boundaries
          
          
            
            
          1. Build-time webOnly dated and verified material can become epoch evidence.
            2. 
            
          2. Frozen search cellDocker receives one read-only epoch/source index, a fake historical clock, and blocked outbound egress.
            3. 
            
          3. Host-side agentsAgents may call model providers, but their evidence comes only through the sealed search API.
            4. 
            
          4. Append-only runsEach launch writes a unique manifest, roster, log, predictions, votes, scores and graph metadata.
            5. 
            
          5. Evaluation boundaryLater outcomes grade forecasts; future information is never returned to the forecasting agent.

                        1. Grow the selected frozen library with psbx corpus build --epoch <epoch> --live --max-docs 400.
                          2.

                          3.

                        4. Run swarm is real: 12 sequential OpenRouter votes (2 × each species), shared retrieval, median probability (phase2-e2012-swarm-probe-container). Same key. Keep it at 1 question unless you mean to wait hours (12×50 at 2s/req).
